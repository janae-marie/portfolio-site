Interests and Values

Public Good

At the heart of what I do, I want to spread goodness. If it's really listening during user interviews, deeply considering accesssibility, or critically thinking abouut possible impacts of change, I aim to look out for others. It's where I find value, purpose, and meaning.

Interest: Urban Systems

My mind has opened with Charles Montgomery's "Happy City" and my life hasn't been the same since. I am fascinated by all the ways cities operate: how they grow, emerge, and act as their own organisms; ones that so powerfully impact our day-to-day lives and happiness. It's the passion that started me on my Complex Systems journey!

Thoughtful Growth: Transparent Success and Transparent Failure

What are we intending to grow toward? What is the mission and what does success tangibly look like? Can it happen from th top or must it grow organically over time to utilize emergent propreties?

Growth for growth's sake should be examined as it often comess with unexpcted (or ignored) costs.

Interdisciplinary Thinking

The problems we face today are complex and require deep thinking utilizing all the types of knowledge and perspectives we have at our disposal. It’s integral to stay curious and unbounded by the way things “are” to find sustainable and successful paths forward. Plus - it’s more fun!

It’s why I’m drawn toward being a product manager, I get to wear many hats, think about problems from several perspectives, and work across teams who think so differently.

Web and Tech Interactions

As much as I love thinking about big problems in big ways, my interests usually boil down to how people interact with technology. I’m fascinated by the possibility for good (how might public services be utilized more if it was just easier?) and the psychology behind communicating meaning via a screen or otherwise.
